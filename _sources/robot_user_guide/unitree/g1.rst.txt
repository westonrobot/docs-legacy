1. Overview
===========

.. image:: figures/G1_robot.png
    :width: 500 px
    :align: center

The G1 is a humanoid robot that stands 130 cm tall and weighs 35 kg. It comes equipped with a LIVOX MID-360 LiDAR and an Intel RealSense D435i depth camera.
